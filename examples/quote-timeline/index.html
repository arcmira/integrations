What you are looking at

These are two short, exact transcript excerpts from a saved Arcmira search, captured October 8, 2026. The query was global, not limited to one channel. It used August 1 inclusive through September 1 exclusive, in UTC, and returned three results. The response’s as-of value was August 25, 2026 at 22:17:26 UTC.

Coverage is partial and results are recency-boosted. This fixture shows two of those results. The third source became private on YouTube and was removed from playback on October 11, 2026. It shows these saved results only. It is not a ranking, a complete survey or a performance benchmark. A live search may return different results.

Transcript wording is preserved, including repetitions. Channel names are not speaker attribution. Speaker identities were not independently checked. Each source link starts at the returned passage timestamp; the short excerpt may begin later inside that passage.

The embedded YouTube player plays the original source with sound. Selecting a card stops the previous source and cues the new passage. This page uses saved search results and makes no Arcmira API calls. Inspect the public fixture · Read the API docs
